Domus

Support

Domus is a private household app for two adults: a shared calendar and duty roster, a project board, family logs, a document vault and a memories album, all kept in your own iCloud.

Need help or found a problem?
Open an issue on the Domus issue tracker and describe what happened. Please do not include personal details from your household.

Getting started

  1. Install Domus on both iPhones and make sure each is signed in to iCloud.
  2. On the first phone, name the household and add the adults and any children.
  3. Open Settings (tap your avatar) → Invite your partner and send the link in Messages.
  4. Your partner taps the link, Domus opens, and from then on every change syncs both ways.

Duty roster widget

Long-press the home screen, tap +, search for Domus and add the Duty roster widget. Long-press the widget and choose Edit Widget to pick whose roster it shows.

Common questions

Sync is not working. Check both phones are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on, and that both are running the same version of Domus. Settings → Sync inside the app shows the current status.

The weather chip is missing. Allow location access for Domus in iOS Settings; the chip hides itself when weather cannot be fetched.
