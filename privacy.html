Domus

Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026

In short: Domus keeps everything you enter in your own iCloud account. The developer does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services.

What Domus is

Domus is a private household app for two adults to run a home together: a shared calendar and duty roster, a project board, family logs, a document vault and a memories album.

Where your data lives

Everything you add to Domus (calendar events, shifts, projects, logs, documents, photos, videos and voice memos) is stored on your device and in the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account using Apple's CloudKit service. When you invite your partner, Domus uses CloudKit sharing so that one other Apple Account can read and write the same household. Nobody else, including the developer, can access it.

The home-screen widget reads a small copy of the duty roster stored on your device only.

Information the developer collects

None. Domus has no developer-operated servers. It does not use analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind.

Permissions Domus asks for

  • Location (while using the app) — to show today's weather on the dashboard. Your location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to fetch the forecast and is not stored by Domus.
  • Camera and photo library — to add photos, videos and documents to your household. They are saved only to your iCloud database.
  • Notifications — to tell you when your partner has made changes. Notifications are generated on your device from your own iCloud data.

You can change any of these at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Weather data

Weather is provided by Apple Weather. See the Apple Weather attribution and data sources.

Children

Domus is intended for adults. Information about children is entered only by the adults in the household and stays in their iCloud account.

Deleting your data

Delete your data from within the app, or remove Domus and its iCloud data under Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Domus. Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing further to delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised on the Domus support page.
